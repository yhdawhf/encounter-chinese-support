Encounter Chinese Privacy Policy

Encounter Chinese is a local-first learning app. It does not require an account, show advertising, track users, or send learning data to the developer or an external server.

Information stored on the device

The app stores the words, example contexts, notes, learning level, and review history that the user adds or generates. This information stays on the user's device in the app's private storage and shared App Group container. The shared container lets the main app, its widget, and its Share extension work with the same learning data.

Clipboard and Share extension

The app reads text from the clipboard only after the user taps Paste. The Share extension receives only the text the user explicitly chooses to share with Encounter Chinese. The app uses that text on the device and does not transmit it to the developer.

Analytics and third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Learning data remains on the user's device until the app's stored data is removed. Deleting Encounter Chinese removes its local data when iOS removes the app and its associated shared container.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the new practices take effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the Encounter Chinese support tracker.
